# Landing Page Section — Product Modules

**Instructions for the builder/tool:** This section introduces the 6 core modules of the platform. Audience is practicing Chartered Accountants and firm partners — not developers, not general SaaS buyers. Copy should read like it was written by someone who has sat across from a CA during a scrutiny notice, not like a SaaS feature list. Avoid AI-hype language ("revolutionary," "game-changing," "powered by AI" as a selling point on its own) — CAs are skeptical of AI tools; lead with the professional outcome, mention AI only as the mechanism.

**Section goal:** Visitor should finish this section understanding "this replaces the six different messy tools/habits I currently use" — not "this is a list of features."

**Layout:** 6-card grid (3x2 on desktop, stacked on mobile). Section headline + one-line subheadline above the grid. Each card: module name, one-line tagline, 2-3 sentence description, and a small "built for" use line. Order below is deliberate — lead with the highest-urgency, highest-differentiation module first, not build sequence.

**Section headline (use as-is or adapt):**
> Six ways this replaces what's currently scattered across WhatsApp, Excel, and a filing cabinet

**Subheadline:**
> Every module runs on the same client file — nothing lives in a silo, nothing gets re-typed twice.

---

## Card 1 — Scrutiny

**Tagline:** Draft notice responses grounded in the client's actual records, not a template.

**Description:** Upload a GST or income tax notice and it's read, categorized, and cross-checked against everything already on file for that client — past filings, invoices, ledgers. A response draft comes back with every claim traceable to a source document. You review, edit, sign off. Nothing goes out without your approval.

**Built for:** The firm that's tired of starting every notice response from a blank page at 11pm before a deadline.

---

## Card 2 — Statutory Calendar

**Tagline:** Every client's filing deadlines, in one place, filled in automatically.

**Description:** GST returns, TDS, advance tax, ROC filings — mapped per client based on entity type, and auto-updated the moment a relevant document lands. No more re-typing a due date into three different trackers. One dashboard shows what's due this week across the entire client roster.

**Built for:** The partner who currently checks five spreadsheets to know what's due tomorrow.

---

## Card 3 — Working Papers

**Tagline:** Upload a document once. Every number in it becomes usable everywhere else.

**Description:** Invoices, ledgers, bank statements, and challans are read automatically — figures extracted, verified, and organized per client. No manual data entry, no re-keying numbers into a calculator. What used to take an associate an afternoon takes minutes, and every extracted figure is checked against the source page.

**Built for:** The associate who spends more time typing numbers into Excel than actually reviewing them.

---

## Card 4 — Correspondence

**Tagline:** Stop chasing documents over WhatsApp.

**Description:** Send a client a single link listing exactly what's needed — they upload, you see what's missing in real time, and they approve the final return with one click before it's filed. Everything is timestamped, so "I sent it last week" is never a guessing game again.

**Built for:** The firm losing hours every month to "did you get my documents?" back-and-forth.

---

## Card 5 — Counsel

**Tagline:** Ask a question about a client, get an answer with the receipts.

**Description:** "What's this client's effective tax rate over the last three years?" gets answered from their actual filed history — not a guess. Every answer cites the exact document and page it came from. If the records don't have the answer, it says so, instead of making one up.

**Built for:** The moment a client asks a sharp question on a call and you need the real number, not "let me check and get back to you."

---

## Card 6 — Bench

**Tagline:** See what your team is carrying before a deadline sneaks up on you.

**Description:** Task assignment, workload, and client health — in one view for the partner running the firm. A client with an overdue filing, an unread notice, or a stalled document request shows up before it becomes a fire drill, not after.

**Built for:** The partner managing more clients than they can hold in their head at once.

---

## Copy rules to keep consistent if adapting/expanding

- Every "Built for" line should name a specific moment of pain, never a generic persona ("CAs who want efficiency").
- Never use the word "AI" as the subject of a sentence in card copy ("AI drafts your response" → "a draft comes back grounded in..."). Keep AI in the mechanism, not the pitch.
- Keep each card under 3 sentences of description. If it needs a 4th sentence to explain, the module isn't simple enough yet — fix the module copy, don't lengthen the card.
- Order is fixed: Scrutiny first (urgency + differentiation), Bench last (least frequently used, most "nice to have once you're bigger").
